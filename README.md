# BioCompute - Research Lab

> **Biology • Computing • Intelligence**

---

## Overview

BioCompute Research Lab explores the intersection of **Biology, Computing, and Intelligence**.

We investigate biological systems not only as subjects of computational study, but as potential **computational substrates** capable of storing, processing, sensing, adapting to, and transforming information.

Our work spans multiple biological scales — from **DNA and molecules to cells, neural systems, living systems, and Bio-AI**.

The central question driving our research is:

> **What if biology itself could become part of the next generation of computing?**

BioCompute Research Lab is associated with **ARL Matrix**, an ecosystem supporting research, experimentation, and emerging technology.

## 💡 Projects

BioCompute Research Lab develops and investigates experimental projects at the intersection of biological systems and computation.

Projects may include:

- Computational models and simulations
- Biological data-processing systems
- DNA and molecular computing experiments
- Biological neural and cellular computing models
- AI-assisted biological research
- Experimental computational architectures
- Research tools and software

Each project is developed with the objective of moving from **concept → research → prototype → engineering → application**.

> Projects will be documented as they mature and become ready for public visibility.

## 🔬 Research

Our research is organized around six interconnected domains.

### 🧬 01 — DNA & Biological Storage

Exploring DNA and other biological molecules as information-storage substrates.

Areas of interest include:

- Information encoding
- Error correction
- Data representation
- Molecular information storage
- Retrieval and decoding
- Computational optimization

---

### 🧪 02 — Molecular Computation

Investigating computation performed through molecular and biochemical systems.

Areas of interest include:

- Molecular logic
- Biochemical reaction networks
- DNA/RNA computation
- Molecular information processing
- Reaction-based computation
- Computational modelling

---

### 🧠 03 — Biological Computation

Exploring biological neural systems and other biological architectures as computational systems.

Areas of interest include:

- Neural computation
- Biological neural networks
- Brain-inspired computation
- Neural information processing
- Hybrid biological-computational architectures

---

### 🦠 04 — Programmable Cells

Investigating cells as programmable systems capable of sensing inputs, processing biological signals, and producing controlled responses.

Areas of interest include:

- Cellular information processing
- Biological logic
- Synthetic biological circuits
- Cellular computation
- Computational modelling and optimization

---

### 🌱 05 — Living & Adaptive Systems

Studying computation that emerges from biological systems interacting with their environments.

Areas of interest include:

- Biological sensing
- Adaptation
- Distributed biological systems
- Emergent computation
- Environmental response
- Collective biological behaviour

---

### 🤖 06 — AI + Biology

Applying artificial intelligence and machine learning to biological computing research while exploring biological inspiration for intelligent systems.

Areas of interest include:

- AI-assisted biological design
- Computational discovery
- Biological-system modelling
- Optimization
- Pattern recognition
- Bio-AI systems

## 🧭 Our Research Path

Our research follows a continuous progression:

<div align="center">
   ```text
Research
   ↓
Model
   ↓
Experiment
   ↓
Prototype
   ↓
Engineer
   ↓
Scale
   ↓
Technology```
</div>

Across our research domains, we explore a broader computational progression:
<div align="center">
   ```text
STORE
  ↓
COMPUTE
  ↓
SENSE
  ↓
LEARN
  ↓
ADAPT
  ↓
INTEGRATE
  ↓
INTELLIGENCE```
</div>

The objective is not to make biology imitate conventional computing.

Instead, we ask:

> **What computational capabilities are possible when biology itself becomes part of the architecture?**
